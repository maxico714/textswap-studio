How matching works

Digital PDFs reuse the embedded font file, size, colour and origin. Scans and images are matched from glyph shape and measured ink; use Detect font and download a family for a closer fit.

Pixel-perfect identity cannot be guaranteed on arbitrary scans or damaged documents.
